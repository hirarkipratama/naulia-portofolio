## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a colorful minimalist visual identity tailored for an elevated, design-forward creative portfolio. It intersects architectural precision with a vibrant, cheerful warmth. Designed for creative technologists, product designers, and multidisciplinary makers, the aesthetic communicates unapologetic polish, creative agility, and distinct Gen-Z professionalism.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on:
- **Architectural Minimalism:** Generous white space, deliberate baseline alignments, structured framing, and disciplined grid disciplines.
- **Chromatic Warmth:** Anchored by dynamic coral-peach tones supported by sun-drenched yellows, crisp mints, and airy periwinkles to break traditional portfolio monotony without forfeiting rigor.
- **Tactile Softness:** Clean surfaces grounded on warm alabaster, pairing featherweight hairline borders with diffuse, ambient warm-tone drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette establishes an upbeat yet curated canvas. Coral Peach serves as the dominant signature hue, delivering an immediate high-energy focal point. Supporting accents inject playful variety across case studies, meta tags, and interactive states.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#FF8E72` / Coral Peach):** Key call-to-actions, active indicators, interactive focal points, and statement elements.
- **Secondary (`#FFD028` / Sunny Yellow):** Highlight moments, decorative backings, badge accents, and celebratory micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#7EE8C2` / Mint Aqua):** Success confirmations, availability badges, and interactive category tags.
- **Accent Soft Sky (`#D0E3FF`):** Secondary metadata tags, tertiary cards, and subtle hovering tint states.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFFDF7` base, `#FFFFFF` cards, `#F0EDE4` borders):** Organic alabaster foundation balancing pure white elevated surfaces and hairline borders.
- **Typography Neutrals (`#1C1A17` primary, `#6B655B` secondary):** High-contrast warm charcoal and muted espresso ensuring crisp optical legibility without the harshness of pitch black.

## Typography

The typographical pairing combines geometric expressiveness with contemporary editorial discipline.

- **Display & Headlines (`Outfit`):** Geometric, buoyant, and structural. Outfit delivers friendly, modern confidence in large scales while preserving clear architectural letterforms. Tighter letter spacing on display scales creates cohesive, striking headline units.
- **Body & Accompanying Microcopy (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Highly legible, humanistic, and contemporary sans-serif. It counterbalances Outfit's roundness with clean vertical metrics, open apertures, and crisp rendering for extended project descriptions and case studies.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** Rendered in upper or title case with deliberate tracking (+0.04em to +0.06em) using medium and bold weights of Hanken Grotesk, maintaining clarity at small screen scales.

## Layout & Spacing

This layout adheres to a structured, 12-column fluid grid system across desktop viewports, consolidating to a 6-column grid on tablets, and a 4-column flow on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max-width containment at `1280px`, outer margins of `3rem` (`48px`), gutters of `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** Outer margins of `2rem` (`32px`), gutters of `1.25rem` (`20px`).
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** Outer margins of `1.25rem` (`20px`), gutters of `1rem` (`16px`).

### Rhythms and Hierarchy
- Content containers employ a base-8 rhythm for all internal component spacing (`space-xs` through `space-xl`).
- Grid cards frequently employ asymmetric spans (e.g., 7 columns for interactive case study showcases, 5 columns for project narrative summaries) to break sterile repetition and sustain visual interest.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses flat architectural planes layered with soft, warm-tinted ambient lighting. Avoid harsh, murky drop shadows; depth is communicative, clean, and cheerful.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Surface):** Flat warm alabaster `#FFFDF7` devoid of shadows.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Case Study Modules, Content Blocks):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface framed with a 1px hairline border in `#F0EDE4`. Drops a soft warm shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(28, 26, 23, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(28, 26, 23, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Active Selection Cards):** Lifts vertically by `2px` to `4px`, transitioning shadow to: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(255, 142, 114, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(28, 26, 23, 0.03)`. The subtle coral warmth in the shadow reinforces brand identity on interaction.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Floating Navigation Bars, Popovers):** Translucent pure white backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`) with `12px` backdrop blur, crisp border `#F0EDE4`, and an expansive ambient lift: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(28, 26, 23, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a Level 2 curvature profile. Corners balance playful consumer friendliness with refined editorial restraint:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Input fields, inline code snippets, small buttons, and action icons.
- **Medium Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Secondary cards, media embeds, project preview thumbnails, and dropdown flyouts.
- **Large Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Hero cards, primary featured project surfaces, modals, and container panels.
- **Pill Form factor (`9999px`):** Category tags, status pills, micro-badges, and main CTA buttons, producing an energetic, inviting finish.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`roundedness: 9999px`), background in Coral Peach (`#FF8E72`), text in `#FFFFFF`, font `Hanken Grotesk` Medium (`0.9375rem`), vertical padding `0.875rem`, horizontal padding `1.75rem`. Hover state: subtle scale up (`1.02`), tone shifts to a richer coral, and coral ambient drop shadow engages.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Pure white background, hairline border in `#F0EDE4`, text in Warm Charcoal (`#1C1A17`). Hover state triggers a subtle background shift to `#FFFDF7` and border highlight in `#FF8E72`.
- **Icon Button:** Circular (`roundedness: 9999px`), fixed size `44px × 44px`, housing center-aligned SVG glyphs, equipped with an expansive hit target.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill radius, padding `0.375rem 0.875rem`, font `label-md`. Color combinations:
  - *Available for Work:* Mint Aqua (`#7EE8C2`) base at 20% opacity with solid `#1C1A17` text and a pulsing solid `#7EE8C2` dot.
  - *Case Study Tag:* Soft Sky (`#D0E3FF`) background with `#1C1A17` text.
  - *Spotlight Badge:* Sunny Yellow (`#FFD028`) background with `#1C1A17` text.

### Cards
- **Project Showcase Card:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with `1.5rem` (`24px`) border radius. Includes a `1px` border in `#F0EDE4`. Image container uses a nested `1rem` (`16px`) radius, creating structured inset alignment.
- **Hover Micro-interaction:** The card glides `3px` upward with a soft coral-tinted ambient shadow. The project title color shifts subtly toward `#FF8E72`.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Text Inputs:** Height `52px`, `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius, `#FFFFFF` surface, hairline border in `#F0EDE4`. Typography uses `body-md`. Focus transitions to an outline in Coral Peach (`#FF8E72`) with a soft matching glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 142, 114, 0.2)`).

### Navigation & Header
- **Floating Island Nav:** Fixed pill dock centered horizontally at bottom on mobile or floating top-center on desktop. Uses blurred white glassmorphism (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), hairline border, and interactive navigation tokens with animated indicator dots.
